Company Story

FreshHarvest started with a simple mission: make fresh, quality groceries accessible to everyone within minutes. What began as a small local delivery service in Mumbai has grown into a trusted premium grocery platform serving 25+ cities.

We partner directly with local farms and certified organic suppliers to ensure every product meets our strict quality standards.
